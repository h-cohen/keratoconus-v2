The project went through experiments E00–E29 under a protocol declared in kcref/PROTOCOL.md. Every run is logged in kcref/results/experiments.csv, which holds 178 rows and 154 distinct run names (some rows are accidental re-runs or repeated scripts, kept under the never-delete rule). The lockbox was scored once (2026-11-06) and dropped by user decision on 2026-11-12. The earlier submitted 0.708 result is superseded because XGBoost early stopping used the test fold, CV was split by eye rather than by patient, 53 of 220 positive scans were taken after the eye was already cross-linked, and units were scans but were called eyes.

Unless stated, AUC is the pooled out-of-fold AUC with a patient-cluster bootstrap 95% CI; "first" is the first-visit AUC. "Dev" means the 713-scan development set; "all" means all 944 scans. "Age" is the age-only logistic regression.

What did not help

  • Better single-eye tabular models: engineered indices, spline age and monotone trees reached 0.595–0.628 against age 0.656 (E01).
  • Image features of any kind on a single eye: ResNet-18, DINOv2 (E04 0.656), spatial and colour grids (E10a 0.663), decoded map indices (E17 0.612–0.630) and a self-supervised autoencoder (E19 0.614–0.628) were at or below age.
  • Prior-scan history: 0.495–0.578 in E03 and 0.589–0.626 in E11, with only 27 and 52 positives, so underpowered rather than proven null.
  • More single-eye training data: 1,487 extra exams raised training positives from 167 to at most 466, yet E18 stayed at 0.613–0.652.
  • Fancier encoders and learners at the end: fine-tuned encoder L got stack weight about 0.04–0.05 (E23); position-aware SSL encoders matched R (E27); no E28 single learner beat LightGBM.
  • Mirrored map differences, tried twice (E14, E15), did not carry over to the all-data model (E15a 0.685 vs E10c 0.697).

What carried the gain

  • The fellow eye at the same visit: E20n nested 0.761 vs age 0.661 (Δ +0.101 [+0.042, +0.160]); single-eye pool stayed at 0.644 (E16a). In E29, L1→L2 is +0.094 [+0.047, +0.144] first visit in noH.
  • The signal is the patient's own fellow eye: a random patient's fellow eye gave 0.633 (E22 negative control); fellow-derived numerics are the largest information source in the noH final model, about 37% of the summed mean |SHAP| over sources (E29).
  • Fellow-eye treatment history (block H) adds +0.049 [+0.020, +0.076] pooled in E22; the label-free noH arm reached 0.731 (E22n_noH) and remains the primary result.
  • Nested selection kept the estimates honest (post-hoc E13 was about 0.01–0.02 optimistic, E16); with the full bilateral feature set, nested and stacked AUC rose to 0.761–0.795 (E20n, E22n, E23n_H). The E28 rank blend added about +0.010 first visit over its LightGBM member (not significant).
  • Training on all scans rather than a bilateral-only specialist: E13 scored 0.785 on the bilateral scans against 0.747 for the specialist (E14c).
  • Hand-crafted map measurements (R) matched every learned image block (E25 noH 0.757, first 0.780), and on top of the fellow eye add a small, borderline increment (first visit +0.017, CI lower bound +0.001; E29).
